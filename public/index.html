Why is this WordPress site slow?

PageSpeed tells you the server is slow. wp-doctor tells you why, and whether your real visitors get the cache: with the consent cookie, with utm_* parameters, on a phone.

      What it checks

      • Page cache, the way real visitors arrive: a repeat visit, a visit with the cookie of the site's own consent banner, a visit from an ad or newsletter link, a visit from a phone.
      • Other pages and missing pages: a few pages from the sitemap, and whether 404s are cached for too long.
      • The page itself: server response time, compression, viewport tag position, main image priority, render-blocking files, reCAPTCHA on pages without forms, plugin files.

      wp-doctor reads public pages like a visitor, identifies itself as wp-doctor, sends about twenty requests one after the other, and keeps nothing: no accounts, no cookies, no trackers.
